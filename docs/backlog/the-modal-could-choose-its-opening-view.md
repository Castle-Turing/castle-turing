# The modal could choose its opening view

**What.** Task 0034 fixed the chord's opening view at compose, on the
argument that the chord carries no information about why it was
pressed. That is true of the chord — but not of the moment: a blocking
question filed twenty seconds ago, an approval the resident was just
notified about, a compose window opened five times today for related
requests — a surface that knew those things could open where the
resident was going anyway. The resident named the boundary while
declining to build this now: intelligence in the default is welcome,
**Clippy is not**. The working distinction from that conversation: a
surface may act on information it was *given* (provenance, pending
state, its own usage), and must not volunteer help nobody asked for or
interrupt to be smart. Static facts shown quietly (0034's `N ready`
line) are on the right side; guessing intent and leading with it is on
the wrong side.

**Why it can wait.** The wrong default view costs one `Tab`. Anything
adaptive here should also probably wait for the resident model to be
readable by more seats ([[workers-do-not-read-the-resident-model]]) so
"adapts to me" is one mechanism, not per-surface heuristics.
