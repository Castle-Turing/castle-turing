# How is demonstrated competence measured, and how fast does it stale?

*A research question, not deferred implementation. Citations from
memory; **[verify]** marks the unconfirmed.*

**What.** `docs/vision.md` commits to competence-gated authority: "a
real, bounded task handed over with coaching, scope widening only as
demonstrated performance earns it … what the resident has done, not
what they've read, is what calibrates how much review a domain still
needs." Its own closing open question asks: "How is a resident's
demonstrated competence actually measured and stored per domain — and
does that one signal drive both explanation altitude and the authority
taxonomy, or do the two need separate mechanisms?"

**The question.** Can competence be validly inferred from opportunistic
performance on whatever real errands happen to occur, or does
trustworthy gating require *designed* assessments — bounded tasks
constructed to be informative? And does a competence judgment, once
made, stay valid, or does skill decay force currency semantics —
entries that expire without recent exercise?

**Where the answer likely lives.** Medical education's *entrustable
professional activities* (ten Cate, 2005 onward) are a near-exact
structural match: supervision level formally tied to demonstrated
performance of defined activities, on a scale from observe-only through
unsupervised **[verify the scale details]**. Notably, medicine uses a
*single* supervision scale for both teaching and authority — which is a
direct answer to the vision's open question about whether one signal
can drive both altitude and the taxonomy. Aviation currency and recency
rules (the 90-day passenger-carrying rule) as institutionalized decay
handling. Skill decay: Arthur, Bennett et al. meta-analysis (1998)
**[verify]**. Knowledge tracing (Corbett & Anderson 1994 and
descendants) for inferring mastery from performance streams. Mastery
learning (Bloom, including the 1984 two-sigma paper). The Dreyfus model
— widely used, widely criticised; useful as a map of the vocabulary.

**What would change.** Resident-model competence entries gain
decay/currency semantics — provenance already exists; *expiry* does
not. The "doing, not being shown" gate might need occasional designed
tasks rather than purely opportunistic ones.

**Priority: medium.** The thread is active, but the mechanism builds
after the taxonomy document. [[authority-taxonomy-prior-art]] and
[[delegation-atrophy]] both feed it.
